# Profile assets

- `tianjin-university.png`: transparent white university mark downloaded from the [official Tianjin University website](https://www.tju.edu.cn/images/tjulogo.png), 2026-09-28. University identity belongs to Tianjin University; used here to identify educational affiliation, not endorsement.
- `tianjin-university-blue.png`: light-background derivative preserving the original alpha channel and geometry; generated by `scripts/render-profile.py`.
- `robotics-loop.gif`, `robotics-preview.png`: original procedural illustration generated by `scripts/render-profile.py` using Pillow and DejaVu Sans. The arm animation is decorative, not a recorded robot experiment.
- Skill badges: Shields.io. The public github-readme-stats endpoint timed out during verification, so external statistics cards are not embedded.

Regenerate local assets with `python3 scripts/render-profile.py` (Pillow required, DejaVu Sans at the script's Linux font path).
